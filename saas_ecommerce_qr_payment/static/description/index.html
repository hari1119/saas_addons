SaaS eCommerce QR Payment Proof

This module helps small businesses sell products through an Odoo website on their own domain, collect manual QR payments, and let internal users verify the customer payment screenshot before confirming the sale order.

Author: Hari
Portfolio: Hari Prasath on LinkedIn

Project Scope

Website Sales

Uses standard Odoo eCommerce. Products published from the product master are available in the website shop.

Separate Domain

Each business can use the Odoo Website domain setting, for example https://shop.example.com.

Manual QR Payment

Shows a QR code or manual bank instructions on the checkout payment page.

Internal Verification

Orders stay pending until a user verifies the uploaded screenshot and confirms the sale order.

Configuration

  1. Install website_sale and this module.
  2. Open Website > Configuration > Settings.
  3. Select the required website at the top of the settings page.
  4. Set the website domain in the standard Domain field.
  5. Enable QR Payment Proof.
  6. Enter the UPI ID and payee name, or enter manual bank instructions.
  7. Publish products from the product master by enabling Can be Sold and publishing them on the website.

Customer Flow

  1. Customer adds products to cart and proceeds to checkout.
  2. Customer scans the QR code or follows the manual payment instructions.
  3. Customer uploads the payment screenshot or PDF proof.
  4. The order is marked as Pending Verification.
  5. The customer sees a confirmation message that the payment proof is under review.

Internal User Flow

  1. Open eCommerce > Orders > QR Payment Proofs.
  2. Review the uploaded screenshot and payment reference on the sale order.
  3. Click Verify QR Payment to confirm the sale order.
  4. Click Reject QR Payment if the payment cannot be matched.
Product listing, checkout, customer records, quotation, sale order confirmation, invoicing, and delivery continue to use standard Odoo eCommerce and Sales behavior.
